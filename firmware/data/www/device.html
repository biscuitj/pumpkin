Update Firmware

Upload a firmware.bin (from firmware/.pio/build/esp32dev/ after pio run). It goes into the backup slot; after the restart the clock checks itself for about 15 seconds. If the new firmware doesn't come up healthy, the clock goes back to this version by itself.
